Open a proposal for work the root sends here: work the human has not directly requested, or a direct request that moves money, changes an account, or sends or publishes outside. Give a short title and a request that explains the proposed work and why; quote a session's words when relevant. The request is the human-facing card text: use the human's language, at most 400 characters, and no internal IDs. The card has two buttons. Set yesLabel to a short action in the human's language, such as 好, 去做 or 发出去, and noLabel to 取消 or 先不做; without them each app shows its own default yes and no labels in the human's interface language. A chat reply leaves the card open. When the work drives apps on this Mac, list them in desktopApps; the card shows them, and yes lets this ask's todos use only those apps. A new app later needs a new proposal. Do not supply todos: wait for the human to answer yes, then add them with neo.todo.add. No drops the ask. A goalId links a cycle proposal to a goal; even a confirmed mandate does not approve the proposal. A retry returns the same proposal and cannot add todos. For any other direct human message, use neo.todo.add with its input instead. A session request records a reply todo; to act on it, open a separate proposal. projectId names the project by id or title. Reuse an open ask for the same matter. topic names a lasting area in the human’s language (2–6 characters). Reuse an existing topic or use 其他.
