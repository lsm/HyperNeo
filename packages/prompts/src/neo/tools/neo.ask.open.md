id: neo.ask.open
---
Record a human request as an ask: a short title, request in the human's words, and optionally its todos (as in neo.todo.add; give every step that needs work an instruction and target). Every todo names what it waits for in after: [] for nothing, or earlier todos by their index in this call or by id; a todo without after is refused. Any daemon session opens asks; goalId links work in a goal cycle to a goal any session owns. projectId names the project by its id (with or without the project: prefix) or its title; an unknown one is refused with the list of projects. A retry in the same turn returns the same ask. Reuse an open ask for the same request instead of opening another: a second ask for a matter that one of your open asks already covers is refused with ask_open and that ask's id, so add the todos there. topic is a lasting area of the human's life or work in their language, 2-6 characters, such as a project, a child, money, the home or health (家长控制, 大学申请, 邮件), not a single matter; reuse a topic from the topic list in your open plans and add one only for a new area; use 其他 when no area fits.
