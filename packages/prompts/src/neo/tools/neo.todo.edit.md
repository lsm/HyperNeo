Change a todo on an open ask you own: title or check {pack, kind, params}; the completion sentence is generated from the check, or reword it directly with doneWhen. addUrls appends PR URLs to its PR-merge check. Duplicate claims and URLs unsupported by the check are recorded without blocking the edit. Prefer editing to adding a duplicate PR todo. For multi-PR work, use one PR per todo with its pack merge check; addUrls names only the PR for that todo once opened. Give each later todo after with the previous todo id so the queue starts it after that todo finishes. Or record completion evidence (done met for what you read, reported for a claim you cannot read); without a passed outcome check it stays open, blocked by its check, and finishes by itself once the check passes; give a working document or evidence file as its absolute path on the owning daemon with one sentence of evidence, not the pasted document. Preview only files on this daemon. Remote files return owner_unavailable; identify their owner rather than re-publishing their paths as local. The artifacts field remains for existing saved attachments and fixed-head Git references. A todo with an unanswered card is refused until the human answers it. Worker final-line blockers are recorded automatically: needs_human first wakes Neo: answer the worker and continue if you can; otherwise ask the human with neo.question.ask (a choice with one option to confirm or several to choose, or a reply) linked to the todo. A blocker you leave alone is never shown to the human: it wakes you again until you resolve it. Handle environment blockers by retrying or escalating their cause. To hold or release a todo use neo.todo.hold. To continue a step whose prerequisite was removed, give it after without that prerequisite. priority changes where waiting work starts in the queue. A check-only todo queued without instruction or target waits on its check: the daemon polls it, and if it has not passed 24 hours after what it waits for finished, the next step is yours. Give it both when a worker should do it instead, or hold/remove it. instruction with target gives a todo that has not started and has no instruction its work; the queue then starts it like any other step. A start target for a coding todo needs target.place from work.find. A send target requires worker remotes matching the todo repository; unknown remotes are refused.
