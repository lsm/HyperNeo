Set or change the topic on an open ask: a short label naming the matter it belongs to. topic is a lasting area of the human's life or work in their language, 2-6 characters, such as a project, a child, money, the home or health (家长控制, 大学申请, 邮件), not a single matter; reuse a topic from the topic list in your open plans and add one only for a new area; use 其他 when no area fits. Settled asks keep their topic.
