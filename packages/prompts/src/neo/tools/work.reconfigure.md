id: work.reconfigure
---
Change a local HyperNeo worker's model, thinking effort, or both without losing its conversation. Use this when its model hits a rate limit or the human asks for a switch. Take ref from work.find or work.status. Use an available model ID and a thinking level supported by that model. Ask the person to use their chat picker for their own or person-named sessions. A next-turn receipt means the saved settings apply on the next turn. A when-idle receipt means the picker queued the change and will apply it once the current work settles. Tell the human the old and new model and whether the switch is queued. Other drivers and attached daemons return not_supported.
