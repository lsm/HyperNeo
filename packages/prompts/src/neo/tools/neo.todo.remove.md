---
id: neo.todo.remove
---
Remove a todo from an open ask you own, with the reason: this is how to cancel or replace one, started or not; neo.todo.close is only for work really done. Its session stops if it is running, unless the todo only follows a session the human tracked. An invalid replacedBy is recorded while removal proceeds without a replacement link. replacedBy names the todo that replaces it; todos that waited for the removed one wait for the replacement instead. Without a replacement, say what its unstarted dependents do with dependents: continue (they go on without it) or remove (they and the unstarted steps behind them are removed too). Leave dependents out only when the human has not said which: the daemon then asks the human on one card and applies the answer, and nothing behind it starts until then. Dependents that already started keep running. A removed todo stays listed, struck through.
