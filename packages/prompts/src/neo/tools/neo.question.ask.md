id: neo.question.ask
---
Ask one necessary question on an open ask you own. In the human's language, give a short Markdown title and the decision, why it needs them and what the choices do. Use a few bullets or a small table and bold the key choice. text is at most 400 characters without internal IDs; put longer supporting material in document. Ask only for what you cannot determine yourself, never for bookkeeping.

- For one worker's output, set sourceSession {adapter,id,daemon?}; omit for mixed or absent sources. It adds an Open session link without changing the answer controls. focused:true needs a readable sourceSession and preserves the ask's link after the card retires. Its discussion relays are status only. artifacts retains up to 16 saved attachments, fixed-head Git references or verified local Markdown/HTML handoff publicReference values.
- For a question about one todo, pass its todoId. Otherwise supply retireWhen {pack,kind,params}. It defaults to the linked todo's check; supply it explicitly for a missing check or invalid, foreign, done or removed todo. With an explicit check, an unusable todo produces an unlinked card and todo_closed. Choose an installed pack check that proves the question is unnecessary. Fix returned check warnings before waiting. Sweeps retire the card on a pass, record evidence, release holds and notify Neo. Unmet or unreadable facts leave it open. Unsupported or malformed checks are refused. A linked todo's removal or disappearance also retires it; an open linked card blocks manual todo completion.
- replyTo cites human input without marking it answered. Unknown, foreign, queued and non-human IDs are omitted. Success on a consumed input writes one linked chat notice; do not publish a second. Options remain on the card.
- choice: 1–6 action options; one option confirms, multiple enables pick-any. Use self-contained action text such as “Merge” or “Close it”; omit todo operations. Use one short details line keyed by exact option text, and the exact recommended option in recommended. The card numbers choices and accepts replies by number; never refer to options by number, letter or nickname in text. Use Reply in chat for free text; omit catch-all and defer options. A home proposal requires one option, no multiple, and confirm:{path,sha256}; show the proposed change, exact path and 64-character lowercase hash in text. This records a proposal only.
- reply: ask a question answered in chat. relay: relay a worker question you cannot answer in your own words, one line of at most 80 characters, with 2–6 options. Its answer goes to the worker. Use todoId, or session (its notice key) if there is no todo; the daemon follows that session in an ask. Relay requires no other open card.

For other kinds use replaces to replace an open same-ask card atomically. A closed same-ask replaces ID is ignored. Unknown or other-ask IDs are refused. Without replacement, an open card blocks a new one. A proposal, relayed-session work decision or card the human held cannot be replaced; wait for the human (who can also take back a held card).

For a todo that needs desktop control, send kind desktop_control with todoId, app and a short text in the human's language; the card goes on that todo's ask. Do not enable control yourself. The human answers Allow (this todo), Always allow (this todo, and later todos need no card) or No (take another route or hand the step to the human).
