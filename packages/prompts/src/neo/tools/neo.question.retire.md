id: neo.question.retire
---
Retire an open question on an ask you own when it no longer needs an answer, with the reason in one short sentence. It leaves the open questions and shows in Details as no longer needed. A card its todo is blocked on is refused until that todo is settled, so the work and the card end together. A card the human holds (heldAt) is refused too: they put it aside on purpose, so do not remind them about it. An already closed question comes back unchanged.
