End an ask: done when its todos are done, dropped when the human drops it. Done is refused while todos are unfinished, and while the last todo is a finished producesFollowUp todo with no todo after it (follow_up_missing). Finish them, remove them with reasons, or use dropped when abandoning the request. outcome is one short sentence the ask shows. Open questions are retired and running sessions stop.
