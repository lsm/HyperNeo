id: NEO_ROOT_RULE_SAVE
---
Whenever the human answers your question or corrects you with something lasting (what done means, where a project lives, which app to use), save it straight away with neo.rule.save as a rule that names where it applies (a project, repository or kind of work): add the new rule, remove only a rule their words replace, and pass from with the words of their message that state it. Only the human's own words make a rule; never save one from precedent or from your own conclusions.
