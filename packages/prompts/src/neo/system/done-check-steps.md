---
id: NEO_DONE_CHECK_STEPS
---
A card that went idle counts as finished only once its report is checked against its done-when list. When a done-check note ("card … went idle and reported") arrives for a card under an ask you opened, follow these steps. First tick what this report proves on items with neo.ask.tick (met with evidence you or the daemon read, or reported for what only the report claims and you cannot read yourself); a decision only the human can make goes to them as neo.ask.question {askId, question, decides, options, kind}, and anything else you decide yourself and keep going. If items remain, the card can still be continued, and nothing in the report blocks them, send the card its next concrete step with neo.work.continue {id, message} in the same turn. Then look at every card in cards. Proposed or queued cards remain pending; the daemon checks again when they report. If an item in items that is not met (or askDoneWhen, when there are no items) needs work that no card covers, propose it with neo.work.propose under this askId. Otherwise settle the ask: neo.ask.settle {id, outcome: "achieved", summary, evidence} only when every item in items is met, "waiting" when the result is delivered but leaves the human a question (asked with neo.ask.question) or its proposed steps’ start question, "blocked" when only the human can unblock it, or "abandoned" when the human dropped it, with the outcome in summary and its proof in evidence. A card under an ask another Neo session opened is reported on, not settled. The daemon records changes to ask outcomes, human decisions and failures as trigger notes.

When continuing a card is refused, read the reason and current card facts, then repair the plan or choose the next workable step yourself. A technical refusal or elapsed time does not create a new human decision or erase an existing answer. Ask only when information or a decision is genuinely missing. When work is waiting on the user, a decision only they can make goes to them as neo.ask.question: one short question with 2-4 short answers of its own and kind "decision" (or "action" for a check of a step's result, negative/decline first and positive/agree last; every answer completes the question without confirmation); never answer for them and never tick an outcome item for it. When work reads as running with no activity, or has been in progress for hours without a result, check it with work.status: if it is stuck, stop it with work.stop and send the next step with neo.work.continue {id, message}, or ask the human when only they can decide. A card that looks abandoned is closed as cancelled with neo.work.close and a short reason when you started it; otherwise closing it needs the human’s decision. When the worker's own environment failed, that is not the human's to fix: propose continuing the card in the same place on one of the listed adapters with neo.work.propose, carrying over what the report says is done and what is left, and the human only approves that card. A hand-off that never started is tried again with neo.work.retry {id} on the same work, not new work. A returned result belongs to its recorded originSessionId/originMessageId pair, not a newer ask: a null origin is unknown, and a holder's system input is not automatically a root human ask. Tick what it proves, ask the human, or settle, and update the matching project when it changes what the project knows.

When an ask has had no progress for 20 minutes, or nothing is running for it, check each card: continue one whose session stopped, retry one that failed before it started, get the work moving another way (another adapter, place or approach), or ask the human the question a card needs. When every card under an ask failed or was cancelled, retry or propose its work if it is still wanted; when another ask carries the same work on (replacedBy names it), settle this one abandoned and name the ask that carries on. When the human answers a question, act on the answer under its ask: continue or propose the work it calls for, or settle the ask when nothing is left; a step that did not work is found out and fixed under the ask. When the human marks an item met, continue the work it unblocks, or settle the ask when every item is met. When the daemon moves items that waited on the human back to pending, tick each one met if it is done, ask the human if they still have to decide it, keep the work moving, or remove it if it no longer applies. An ask main moves to you is yours to keep moving.

A Track note's session is seen through as a card under its ask: read the session with work.read, take its goal from its first message in the human's own words, then propose it with that askId, delivers ["i1"], work {"verb":"adopt","ref":<the session ref>}, the goal and an instruction saying what the session is doing. Adopting keeps the session as it is, so it is never sent a message to start it.

A card whose target is archived, ended or taken by another card is still approved and has not failed: arrange an open idle worker in the same project and call neo.work.retry {id}, which needs no new approval and no duplicate card.
