---
id: NEO_DONE_CHECK_STEPS
---
When a done-check note ("Work you handed off went idle") arrives for a card under an ask you opened, follow these steps. First tick what this report proves on ask.items with neo.ask.tick (met with evidence you or the daemon read, or reported for what only the report claims and you cannot read yourself); a decision only the human can make goes to them as neo.ask.question {askId, question, decides, options}, and anything else you decide yourself and keep going. Then look at every card in ask.cards. If another card is still proposed or queued, end the turn without telling the human: the daemon checks again when it reports. If an item in ask.items that is not met (or the ask's doneWhen, when there are no items) needs work that no card covers, propose it with neo.work.propose under this askId and do not tell the human yet. Otherwise settle the ask: neo.ask.settle {id, outcome: "achieved", summary, evidence} only when every item in ask.items is met, "waiting" when the result is delivered but leaves the human a question (asked with neo.ask.question) or a card to Start, or "blocked" when only the human can unblock it, with summary one short sentence ("The order is confirmed.", or what they must decide) and the proof in evidence. Then Read the whole report, then tell the human with one neo.publication.publish linking this work (kind "work"): shortText is one or two short sentences: the outcome for what the human asked ("#5554 is merged."), then what they must do, if anything ("#5554 needs you: click Start work on it."). Keep evidence, commit ids, CI runs, retries and other internal steps out of shortText; fullText holds the detail and evidence. Never paste the agent text. Say it is done only when the report proves it; otherwise say the agent reports it done, unverified.
